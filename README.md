**📊 AI Usage and Its Impact on Students and Professionals**

Exploratory Data Analysis | Machine Learning | Deep Learning

📌 Project Overview

This project investigates how Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools influence the productivity of students and professionals.

Using Python, Data Analysis, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning techniques, the project explores the relationship between AI usage, time saved, user satisfaction, and productivity.

The project includes comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), data preprocessing, feature engineering, and implementation of multiple classification algorithms to predict whether an individual belongs to the High Productivity or Low Productivity category.

Different models are trained and evaluated to compare their performance and identify a suitable model for productivity classification.

**🎯 Project Objectives**

* Analyze AI usage patterns among students and professionals.

* Identify the most frequently used AI tools.

* Investigate the relationship between AI usage and productivity.

* Analyze the impact of AI on time savings and user satisfaction.

* Compare productivity scores across different AI tools.

* Perform data cleaning and feature engineering.

* Build Machine Learning classification models.

* Implement a Deep Learning Neural Network.

* Compare training and testing accuracy of different models.

* Identify the most balanced model based on training and testing performance.

**📈 Key Exploratory Data Analysis Findings**

**1. AI Usage and Productivity**

AI usage hours show a positive correlation with productivity.

Correlation: 0.63

This indicates that users with higher AI usage generally report higher productivity scores in this dataset.

**2. Time Saved and Productivity**

Time saved through AI has the strongest positive relationship with productivity.

Correlation: 0.75

Users who save more time through AI generally report higher productivity.

**3. Satisfaction and Productivity**

Satisfaction Score also has a positive relationship with productivity.

Correlation: 0.64

**4. Student vs Professional Productivity**

Professionals  =  5.26

Students  =  5.18

The difference between the two groups is relatively small.

**5. AI Tool Analysis**

ChatGPT is the most frequently used AI tool.

DeepSeek has the highest average productivity score among the analyzed AI tools.
